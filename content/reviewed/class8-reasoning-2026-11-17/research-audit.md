# Sample-paper research audit, 17 November 2026

## Method and limits

Three current discovery searches sampled 'class eight sample paper', 'class eight CBSE sample paper', and 'class eight practice paper', with 10 results each. They returned 23 distinct URLs. All 23 pages were retrieved for triage, including PDF-to-text results where available. This is not a complete personalized Google SERP export or proof of a fixed page-8 rank. Search ordering varies. Unrelated subjects, old books, another board and Olympiad papers were excluded as syllabus models.

A public Delhi Maths PDF was downloaded and inspected directly. A direct download of the DAV paper returned HTTP403; the separate page extraction supplied its text, but no downloadable local copy is claimed. The Busy Brains science paper was read in-page; its PDF is offered only on request, so no contact request or PDF download was made. Some landing pages advertise downloads whose linked PDFs were not all retrieved. No complete competitor archive or answer-key validation is claimed.

- TuitionRocks: Maths and Science chapter practice grouped under Ganita Prakash and Curiosity. https://tuitionrocks.com/worksheets-practice/class-8/

## Public collection findings and recommendations

The public Evater hub offers27chapter papers, each22marks/45minutes, with answers. Its topic/subtype coverage is useful for chapter revision. Generic Class8sample-paper queries also surface longer mixed-chapter papers, so the two intents should be served honestly rather than renaming chapter papers as full exams. Clear subject landing collections and descriptive titles can improve relevance and discovery, without guaranteeing search positions.

Recommended content sequence: useful Maths/Science landing collections; original reasoning-format question expansion; then independently authored mixed-chapter papers with solutions and explicit coverage. School exam patterns differ, so avoid implying an official CBSE pattern or endorsement.
