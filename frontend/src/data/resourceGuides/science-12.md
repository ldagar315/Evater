# Class 8 Science: How Nature Works in Harmony - Watch, Understand and Practise

Pick one topic. Use the video where shown, read the short explanation, then try the question before checking your answer.

## 1. Habitats and ecosystem components

### Watch: What are ecosystems?

KLU App

[Open the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P1X-WpfUvm4)

### Understand it

An ecosystem includes living organisms and non-living conditions such as sunlight, soil, air and water. Instead of making two isolated lists, draw links: a plant uses light and water, while its roots interact with the soil. Explain one link before adding more.

### Try it yourself

Are sunlight and soil part of an ecosystem discussion?

**Check:** Yes. Non-living conditions affect the living community.

## 2. Food chains and food webs

### Think it through

A food chain shows one feeding route; a food web connects several routes. State what your arrows mean. When showing energy transfer, point from the food to the organism that eats it. One consumer can belong to several chains, so changing one population can affect more than one route.

### Try it yourself

Can one consumer belong to more than one food chain?

**Check:** Yes. Interconnected feeding relationships form a food web.

## 3. Decomposers and nutrient cycling

### Watch: How dirt works

The Nature Conservancy

[Open the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K7YpPgbmSew)

### Understand it

Decomposers break down dead material and return materials to the environment. Nutrients can cycle into new living things, while energy flows through an ecosystem and is dissipated. Decomposition can be slower in some conditions, so do not assume every dead organism decays at the same rate.

### Try it yourself

Is energy endlessly recycled through an ecosystem in the same way nutrients can be?

**Check:** No. Energy flows through and is dissipated, while materials can cycle.

## 4. Interdependence and ecosystem change

### Watch: Why is biodiversity so important? - Kim Preshoff

Kim Preshoff

[Open the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GK_vRtHJZu4)

### Understand it

Organisms depend on many other organisms and on their surroundings. Removing a species can affect its food, its predators and other species connected indirectly through the web. Use the links to suggest possible effects, then ask what observations would be needed to test your prediction.

### Try it yourself

Could removing one species affect species it does not eat directly?

**Check:** Yes. Indirect links in a food web can spread the effects.
