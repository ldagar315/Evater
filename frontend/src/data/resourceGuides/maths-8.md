## 1. Fractions, decimals and percentages

### Watch: Fraction, decimal, and percent from visual model

Khan Academy

[Open the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wwg052FC_Zw)

### Understand it

Percent means per hundred. To connect a fraction, decimal and percentage, represent the same quantity in each form. For 3/8, division gives 0.375; multiplying by 100 gives 37.5%. Sketching a shaded model helps you check whether the answer should be less than half.

### Try it yourself

Write 3/8 as a decimal and percentage.

**Check:** 0.375 and 37.5%.

## 2. Finding a percentage of a quantity

### Watch: Percentages without a calculator - Corbettmaths

corbettmaths

[Open the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9QKc5bZPLv0)

### Understand it

Start with a percentage you can find easily. Ten percent of 240 is 24, and five percent is half of that: 12. So fifteen percent is 36. Always identify the whole first; a correct percentage applied to the wrong total still gives the wrong amount.

### Try it yourself

Find 15% of 240.

**Check:** 36: 10% is 24 and 5% is 12.

## 3. Percentage increase and decrease

### Watch: Percentage Change - Corbettmaths

corbettmaths

[Open the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q2gRAS08fE0)

### Understand it

Percentage change compares the change with the original amount. A rise from ₹200 to ₹250 is a ₹50 increase. Divide by the original ₹200: 50/200 × 100% = 25%. Writing the original amount beside your fraction prevents you from using the final price as the base.

### Try it yourself

A price rises from ₹200 to ₹250. What is the percentage increase?

**Check:** 25%, because the increase ₹50 is one quarter of the original ₹200.

## 4. Applying percentages in context

### Think it through

A discount is taken from the marked price. Fifteen percent of ₹800 is ₹120, so the sale price is ₹680 before other charges. Profit or loss percentage usually uses cost price instead. Underline the quantity that counts as the whole before calculating any percentage.

### Try it yourself

A ₹800 item has a 15% discount. What is the sale price before any other charge?

**Check:** ₹680. The discount is ₹120.
