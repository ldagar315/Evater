## 1. The distributive property

### Watch: Expanding Brackets - Corbettmaths

corbettmaths

[Open the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZJA1qz4XovY)

### Understand it

To multiply a whole group, multiply every part: a(b + c) = ab + ac. For 17 × 98, use 98 = 100 - 2. Then 17 × 98 = 1,700 - 34 = 1,666. A split rectangle can show the same calculation as two areas.

### Try it yourself

Calculate 17 × 98 without long multiplication.

**Check:** 17 × (100 - 2) = 1700 - 34 = 1666.

## 2. Products of sums and differences

### Think it through

Every term in the first bracket must multiply every term in the second. For (x + 3)(x + 2), the four products are x², 2x, 3x and 6. Combine the like terms to get x² + 5x + 6. A four-part rectangle helps you check that no product is missing. Label one side x + 3 and the other x + 2; the four smaller areas are x², 2x, 3x and 6. Grouping the two x-terms does not change the area.

### Try it yourself

Expand (x + 3)(x + 2).

**Check:** x² + 5x + 6.

## 3. Squares and difference of squares

### Watch: Squaring a binomial

[Open the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4GcNzvILqtM)

### Understand it

Squaring a sum means multiplying the whole bracket by itself. (a + b)² gives a² + ab + ab + b² = a² + 2ab + b². The two cross-products explain the middle term. For a difference of squares, (a + b)(a - b) = a² - b² because the cross-products cancel.

### Try it yourself

Why is (x + 4)² not x² + 16?

**Check:** It also includes 8x: the two cross-products are 4x and 4x.

## 4. Finding and fixing expansion errors

### Think it through

A number check can expose a wrong expansion, but the algebra explains the fix. In 3(x - 5), the 3 multiplies x and -5, giving 3x - 15. Substituting x = 6 gives 3 on both sides. The incorrect 3x - 5 would give 13 instead. One counterexample is enough to disprove the proposed identity, but matching at one chosen value would not prove an identity for every x. The distributive step supplies the reason it works in general.

### Try it yourself

A student writes 3(x - 5) = 3x - 5. Fix it.

**Check:** 3x - 15. The factor 3 multiplies both terms.
